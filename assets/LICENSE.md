# Where the game's files come from (assets)

| Folder | What | Source and licence |
|---|---|---|
| assets/env | the candle-lit hall: teak top, light map, brass props, hall HDR | made here by tools/env (Blender scripts); nothing downloaded |
| assets/scan | scanned flagstones and rocks for the scenes | Poly Haven, CC0 1.0; see assets/scan/LICENSE.md |
| assets/models | scanned sculptures for the table figures | Poly Haven, CC0 1.0; see assets/models/LICENSE.md |
| assets/fx | particle and light sprites | Kenney, CC0; see assets/fx/KENNEY_LICENSE.txt |

No official Battle of Talingchan art, playmat or card image is stored in this repository: card data and pictures load live from the official CDN.
